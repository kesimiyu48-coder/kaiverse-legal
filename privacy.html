Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Kaiverse Studios Discord AutoModeration bot (“the bot”) processes information when installed in a Discord server.

1. Information processed

To detect prohibited Discord invitation links, the bot processes message content made available to it through Discord. Discord may also provide technical identifiers, such as the message, user, and server IDs, so the bot can identify and act on the relevant message.

2. How information is used

Message content is checked for prohibited invitation links. When a message matches the bot’s configured rule, the bot deletes that message. The bot is not intended to use message content for advertising, profiling, or unrelated purposes.

3. Storage and retention

The bot is designed to delete matching messages without intentionally keeping a database or moderation-history record of the deleted message or the user who sent it. Message content is processed to perform the detection and deletion function.

Important: This statement describes the bot’s intended behaviour. The bot owner should verify the deployed code, hosting logs, error monitoring, backups, and any third-party services before publishing this policy. Those systems may independently process technical data.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not intentionally disclose message content to third parties for their own advertising purposes. Discord and the infrastructure providers used to operate the bot may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the service to protect information processed by the bot. No internet-based system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Discord and third-party services

Your use of Discord is also governed by Discord’s policies: Discord Privacy Policy and Discord Terms of Service.

7. Your questions and requests

For privacy questions or concerns about information processed by the bot, contact us at kaiversestudiosco@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the bot’s functionality or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
